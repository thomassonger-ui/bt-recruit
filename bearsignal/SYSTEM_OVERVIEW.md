# BearSignal System Overview
BearSignal is a multi-channel marketing automation system built to eliminate manual content creation and posting.

## Problem
Agents do not market consistently due to:
- Lack of time
- Lack of systems
- Lack of content strategy

## Solution
BearSignal automates:
1. Content creation (via Scout)
2. Content formatting per channel
3. Scheduled distribution
4. Campaign sequencing

## Channels
- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- X (Twitter)
- Email

## Output Types
- Posts
- Carousels
- Campaign emails
- Print-ready assets

## Result
Always-on marketing presence.
