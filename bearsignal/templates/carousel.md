# Carousel Template

## Format (5-Slide Standard)
Slide 1 — Hook / Title
Slide 2 — Problem
Slide 3 — Key Insight #1
Slide 4 — Key Insight #2 / Solution
Slide 5 — CTA / Takeaway

## Design Rules
- Bold headline each slide
- Max 3 lines body text per slide
- Bear Team brand colors
- Logo on final slide
- Swipe prompt on slide 1

## Types
- Commission comparison
- Reasons to join Bear Team
- Market stats
- Step-by-step process
